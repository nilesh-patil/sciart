## Shared numerics (crate-private, GEN-R3)

`src/lbm.rs` — the D2Q9 TRT lattice Boltzmann module this crate's two flow scenes share
(formerly `sciart-core`'s CORE-R98/R99; moved back when it turned out to serve one crate).
Velocity set (`CX`, `CY`, `OPP`, `W`), `tau_minus(τ, Λ)`, the shifted equilibrium `heq`
and TRT `collide` free functions, and the SoA `Lattice` with `step_periodic`.

- **Acceptance**: `LBM-A1` the kernel matches the spec text: weights sum to 1, opposite pairs are consistent, `heq`'s zeroth/first moments are `δρ` and `ρu`, an equilibrium node is a fixed point of `collide`, and `collide` conserves mass and momentum to `f32` rounding (proptest). `LBM-A2` a fully periodic `Lattice` step conserves total mass to `f32` rounding, keeps a uniform flow uniform, damps a shear wave at the TRT viscosity within 2 %, bounce-back keeps solids at rest, degenerate sizes never panic, and the step allocates nothing (through GEN-R11's scene suites).

### brownian_motion  (Forces & Waves, M5, M12, M15, M20)
- **Intent**: a large pollen grain jittering among fast solvent molecules, leaving a fading random-walk trail, with a live mean-squared-displacement readout.
- **Model**: grain obeys the Langevin/Ornstein–Uhlenbeck equation `dv = −γ v dt + σ √dt ξ` (γ = 2 s⁻¹, σ chosen so the diffusion constant `D = σ²/(2γ²)` gives ~3 rows/√s), exact OU update for the velocity; reflecting walls; trail of the last 180 positions (`Trail`); `≈ 0.02·area` (max 400) visible solvent molecules moving ballistically with Maxwell–Boltzmann velocities and reflecting walls, occasionally flashing where they "hit" the grain; readout `D^ (MSD@2s) = …  D = … rows^2/s  t = …s` (ASCII, right-aligned, GEN-R13): `D̂` from the time-averaged MSD at a 2 s lag with the exact OU inertial correction (a single grain's `r²/4t` from the start never converges); when it would reach the title trailing parts (t, then the true D) are dropped, never clipped, and the readout is hidden when even `D^ (MSD@2s) = …` does not fit. The joint position–velocity OU transition is sampled exactly; walls are exact specular reflections via folding (method of images).
- **Inks**: grain `Accent`, trail `Structure` fading by age, molecules `Particle`, readout `Text`.
- **Acceptance**: `BRO-A1` the stationary velocity variance per axis converges to `σ²/(2γ)` (seeded statistical test, 5 %); `BRO-A2` the ensemble MSD at time t is `4Dt` within 10 % for t ≫ 1/γ (seeded, many walkers); `BRO-A3` the grain and molecules always stay inside the box; the trail length is ≤ 180.
- **Look (M20)** (GEN-R25, colourful dust and walk thread; supersedes the inks above): same Langevin model, molecules and readout. Drawn on one `raster::Dots` lattice in `prepare`. The grain is an `Accent` stipple atom (`Dots::stipple_puff`, σ = the grain radius in dots ÷ 2.6, peak 1.0: compact, with a solid core). Its random walk (the existing 180-sample `Trail`) is a 1-px braille thread coloured along its length by age, newest to oldest `Hot` → `Sun` → `Particle` → `Wave` → `Cold` in five equal spans, the level falling from 230 to 70, so the path reads as a rainbow ribbon fading into the past. Molecules are single braille dots coloured by speed against the Maxwell–Boltzmann quantiles of their own distribution: the slowest third `Cold`, the middle third `Wave`, the fastest third `Hot`. A molecule that struck the grain within the last 0.3 s (every hit; the M5 flash probability is dropped) is drawn as a 5-dot `Sun` plus sign fading 255 → 150 (the kick flash). The readout stays `Text` on the bottom row.
- **Acceptance** (M20): `BRO-A4` a molecule's role follows its speed by the MB terciles (a pure `speed → role` function: below the 1/3 quantile `Cold`, above the 2/3 quantile `Hot`, else `Wave`), and over a seeded population each role holds 33 ± 5 % of the molecules; `BRO-A5` the walk thread's role is a pure function of sample age, measured against the full 180-sample trail (a colour always means the same age in seconds, so a young trail shows only the newest spans), with five equal spans in the order `Hot`, `Sun`, `Particle`, `Wave`, `Cold`, and its level is non-increasing with age; `BRO-A6` at 200×50 after 5 s no cell is a half-block cell and at least 85 % of lit cells outside the readout/title row are braille.

### chladni_plate  (Forces & Waves, M21)
- **Intent**: sand on a vibrating plate dances off the moving regions and settles on the still lines, drawing a Chladni figure; when the tone changes, the sand scatters and finds the new figure.
- **Model**: a square plate in Chladni's approximation for free-plate modes, `u(x, y) = ½[cos(nπx) cos(mπy) ± cos(mπx) cos(nπy)]` (so `|u| ≤ 1`), over a table of `(m, n, ±)` modes: (1,5,+), (4,6,−), (2,4,+), (2,8,−), (3,5,+), (4,8,−), (2,6,+), (3,8,−), ordered so each figure's nodal lines lie on the next one's lobes. Sand: `N` grains (one per 12 dots² of plate, capped at 3000) take random kicks of `5·|u|` dots per 1/60 s (bouncing off the moving plate, fractional kicks carried over so they follow simulated time); a kick that would land on livelier plate is rejected half the time (a net drift toward the nodes), and kicks under 0.4 dots leave a grain in place (static friction), so grains collect on the nodal lines and hold still; edges reflect. Each mode lasts ~15 s on a looping `Timeline`; at a change the grains are tossed by the new mode.
- **Motion**: grains drift and gather; mode changes scatter and re-gather them.
- **Resize**: the plate is a centred square in dot units (square braille sub-cells) with a blank row above it and one above the title row; grains kept in plate coordinates.
- **Look** *(GEN-R26)*: sand grains as single bright braille dots in `Sun`; the plate's antinode lobes as very sparse fixed-threshold stipple, density `≤ 0.10 · |u|`, `Hot` where `u > 0`, `Cold` where `u < 0` (the static mode shape, so it holds still); the plate edge a 1-px `Dim` thread. Stipple density `0.08·|u|`. Readout right-aligned on row `h − 1`: the mode and its form, `m 3  n 5  +` (`−`, ASCII `-`), when it fits.
- **Title**: `Chladni plate · sand finds nodes`; no captions.
- **Stylisation**: Chladni's product approximation, not the true free-plate eigenmodes; the sand is a random-kick caricature of bouncing grains, with a drift toward the nodes added so it settles in a few seconds.
- **Acceptance**: `CHL-A1` the mode function vanishes on its nodal set and has the symmetry of its `±` form; `CHL-A2` 10 s after a mode change the mean `|u|` at the grains is below 0.15 for 3 seeds (sand on the nodes), and right after the change it is above 0.3; `CHL-A3` no grain leaves the plate; `CHL-A4` plate stipple dots are `Hot` only where `u > 0`, `Cold` only where `u < 0`, and density never exceeds 0.10.

### cloud_chamber  (Forces & Waves, M21)
- **Intent**: a diffusion cloud chamber in a magnetic field: invisible particles leave condensation trails: short fat alpha streaks, thin electrons curling into spirals, long straight muons, now and then a delta ray or an electron–positron pair.
- **Model**: Poisson events at fixed mean rates per species (`ctx.rng`); each track is a polyline integrated once at creation: alpha straight, length from a range distribution, droplets denser at the end (Bragg-like); beta a helix projection in a uniform out-of-screen `B`, radius `r = p / (|q| B)` shrinking as it loses energy, curl direction by charge sign; muon nearly straight across the chamber with rare delta rays (a short beta branch); pair production a `V` of opposite curls. Droplets condense along a track over ~0.1 s, then drift down slowly and fade over ~2 s; fixed pools of track slots and droplets (no allocation after `resize`).
- **Motion**: tracks appear, condense, sag and fade continually.
- **Resize**: chamber scale rebuilt; live tracks rescaled or cleared.
- **Look** *(GEN-R25)*: everything braille. A track is a thread of droplet dots: alpha `Hot` and 2 dots thick, betas `Cold` thin (positrons too, told apart by their curl), muons `Accent` thin, delta rays thin `Cold`. Fading lowers the level in flat steps and thins the dots with fixed per-dot thresholds, so a fading track holds still. Faint `Dim` vapour specks optional (≤ 1 % of dots).
- **Title**: `Cloud chamber · trails of ions`; no captions.
- **Stylisation**: rates and ranges illustrative, not a specific source; tracks are 2-D projections; the magnetic field is exaggerated so betas curl on screen, and the electrons' energy loss is exaggerated so their spirals close on screen; the slight downward sag of old tracks borrows from a side view, while the random track directions are a top view.
- **Acceptance**: `CLD-A1` a beta's curl direction flips with its charge sign, and its initial radius is proportional to its momentum; `CLD-A2` alpha tracks are straight (max deviation ≤ 1 dot) and their lengths fall within the range distribution; `CLD-A3` a track is fully gone within its fade lifetime and its slot reused; `CLD-A4` over 60 s the event counts per species match their mean rates within 3σ for 3 seeds; `CLD-A5` the droplets of a fading track are a subset of its earlier droplets (fixed thresholds; positions sag and diffuse with age).
- **As built**: rates per second at 200×50: alpha 1.2, beta 1.8, muon 0.95, pair 0.15, scaled by `(S/S_ref)^0.4` with the chamber scale `S = √(dot area)`; track lengths and radii scale with `S`, so small chambers stay lively and big ones are not crowded (≈ 6 live tracks at 80×24, 8 at 120×40, 9 at 200×50, 14 at 500×150). Arrivals are Poisson with a floor: when fewer than 4 tracks are live, one extra event (species drawn by rate) arrives at once (≤ 15 % of events at 80×24, ≤ 2 % at 200×50; counted apart from the Poisson events, which CLD-A4 checks). 28 track slots, filled at start with the steady state's mix of species and ages. Alpha ranges from the radon-chain lines (3.9–6.9 cm, 2 % straggling) on a ~40 cm chamber; the field is set so 1 MeV/c curls at radius 0.055·S; betas 0.7–1.5 MeV/c log-uniform (85 % electrons; starting radius ≤ 0.08·S, ≈ 8 cells across at 120×40), loss `dr/ds = −κ(1 + (r_c/r)²)` (κ = 0.11, r_c = 6 dots: ≈ 1.5 turns from the fastest beta to its end) with a multiple-scattering kick ∝ 1/p, droplets every dot along the path (a continuous thread); positive charges curl clockwise on screen (B out of the screen); delta rays 0.2–0.4 MeV/c, leaving at `cos θ = T/p` (70–80°); sag 0.8 dots/s; a `Dim` drizzle on 0.1 % of the dots. Hold 0.7 s, gone at 2.3 s. The roles' astro luma (≈ 0.52) caps the scene's p99 lit luma at ≈ 0.52.

### doppler_effect  (Forces & Waves, M4, M12, M15)
- **Intent**: a moving source compressing wavefronts ahead (higher pitch) and stretching them behind (lower pitch).
- **Model**: wave speed `c = 10` rows/s, source speed `v = 0.5c` left→right (wraps with margin), one wavefront every `T = 0.35 s`, each front a circle of radius `c·(t − t₀)` around its emission point; fronts are dropped once larger than the screen diagonal.
- **Inks**: each front point is coloured by its direction θ from its own emission point — `Cold` (higher pitch, `cos θ > 0`) else `Hot`; on the axis this is exactly "ahead of the source ⇒ `Cold`", and it stays correct off-axis and after the source wraps; source `Accent`. Cold/Hot is a pitch convention, not a colour shift: the model is sound.
- **Acceptance**: `DOP-A1` in steady state the spacing of fronts along the axis is `(c − v)T` ahead and `(c + v)T` behind; `DOP-A2` the number of live fronts is bounded by `diag/(cT) + 2`.

### double_pendulum  (Forces & Waves, M7, M12, M15)
- **Intent**: chaos made visible — two double pendulums released from almost identical angles swing together, then diverge wildly, each tracing a glowing path.
- **Model**: exact Lagrangian equations for point masses `m₁, m₂` on massless rods `L₁, L₂` (rows, aspect-correct on screen), `g` scaled so the upper arm alone has a small-angle period of 2.2 s (the coupled slow mode is ≈ 2.78 s); physics runs in model units (`L₁ = 1`) with a separate screen scale, so motion is identical at any terminal size; both twins share one pivot; state `(θ₁, θ₂, ω₁, ω₂)` advanced with `ode::integrate` at `h ≤ 1/240 s`; the twin starts with `Δθ₂ = 10⁻³` rad; high-energy seeded initial angles (both arms above horizontal ± variety); trails of the lower bobs (`Trail`, ~2 s); a divergence readout (`|Δθ| e-folding`) and energy drift; restart every 45 s with new seeded angles.
- **Inks**: pendulum A `Cold`, B `Hot`, rods `Structure`, pivots `Text`, trails fading by age.
- **Acceptance**: `DP-A1` total energy drifts < 0.5 % over 20 s; `DP-A2` for small angles the two normal-mode frequencies match the linearised analytic values within 1 %; `DP-A3` from a seeded chaotic start the twins' separation (max so far — it saturates near π) grows ≥ 100× within 30 s; `DP-A4` rods keep their lengths exactly (bob positions derived from angles).

### kelvin_helmholtz  (Forces & Waves, M13)
- **Intent**: two layers of fluid sliding past each other: the interface ripples, curls into cat's-eye billows like wave clouds, neighbouring billows pair and merge, and the layer thickens into turbulence before it is reset.
- **Model**: D2Q9 TRT lattice Boltzmann (the vortex_street kernel, now this crate's shared `lbm` helper, LBM-A1/A2) on a capped lattice (≤ 16 384 nodes, the display's square-pixel aspect clamped to [0.75, 4] and bucketed by 1.25), periodic in both axes, so the flow is the classic *double* shear layer `u = U[tanh((y − H/4)/δ) − tanh((y − 3H/4)/δ) − 1]` (no walls to shed their own vorticity; the two rows of billows spin in opposite senses); `U = 0.1`, `τ = 0.508` (Λ = ¼), `δ = max(H/28, 2.5)` (Re = Uδ/ν ≈ 95–200 with the aspect bucket; 121 at 200×50); the node budget is also capped at the display's half-block pixel count (never below 1 200 nodes), so small displays simulate a smaller block with fewer, larger billows instead of a minified one. Each interface is seeded with a small transverse velocity (Gaussian envelope of width 2δ) at the wavenumber nearest `λ ≈ 12δ` with an even count `n` across the width (the fastest-growing mode once the layer has thickened at this resolution) and its subharmonic `n/2` (pairing), phases from `ctx.rng`. A passive dye marks the middle band, advected semi-Lagrangian (clamped Catmull–Rom) with a weak interface sharpening (stylisation). A view whose pixels span more than 1.3 nodes box-filters the picture before sampling. 4 LBM steps per update; 1 s pre-roll in the first resize of a lattice; the act re-seeds after 36 s (before the under-resolved late turbulence can go unstable), or when the watchdog (1 s cadence) finds ρ outside [0.8, 1.2] or |u| ≥ 0.3, or when the row-mean flow keeps less than a quarter of the laid-down shear (the merged vortices have reorganised the block: turbulence, then reset).
- **Motion**: ripples at once, billows by ~3 s, pairing by ~9 s into half as many large cat's-eye vortices, which then turn slowly until the reset.
- **Resize**: within a bucket nothing changes; a new bucket gets its own lattice and a fresh act (the seeded modes depend on the lattice, so resampling the old flow would seed the wrong ones). The first act's 60-update pre-roll runs 15 updates in `resize` and one extra per `update` after it until paid (GEN-R19: ≈ 9 ms instead of ≈ 34 ms at 500×150).
- **Inks** (M15, ADR-0018: M11's complementary pair in flat steps; was a smooth `Ramp(Starlight)` line): lines only, on black. The interfaces, where the passive dye crosses ½, are thin flat lines, the pixels whose distance `d` to the `c = ½` contour (estimated per pixel as `|c − ½| / |∇c|` in pixels) is under 0.9: `Hot` where the sheet's vorticity is positive and `Cold` where it is negative (the two shear layers spin opposite ways, so the upper billows read warm and the lower cool), at 130 and, where |ω| reaches 0.45 of the saturation value, 230. Everything else is the terminal's black (the dye bands are not drawn). Dye, signed spin and slope are sampled bilinearly at any magnification (the line is drawn by its distance, not by the sampling, so a cubic would only double the per-pixel cost at 500×150). A minified display box-filters dye and spin first. ASCII: `#` spinning, `-` calm. Readout `Re 121` right-aligned on the title row when it fits.
- **Title**: `Shear billows · layers roll up`.
- **Stylisation**: 2-D, low Reynolds number, periodic domain; perturbation seeded for a clean first roll-up.
- **Acceptance**: `KH-A1` the shared `lbm` is the one kernel both flow scenes run (vortex_street's private copy was deleted in favour of it with byte-identical snapshots; the kernel itself is pinned by LBM-A1/A2), and the scene steps exactly the shared lattice; `KH-A2` the interface perturbation's amplitude grows exponentially early (log-linear fit R² > 0.9); `KH-A3` the number of vortices (vorticity extrema along the interface) decreases after pairing; `KH-A4` total mass is conserved to 1e-6 relative over 10 s (f32 rounding, LBM-A2); `KH-A5` worst frame < 2 ms at 200×50 on the stepping frame (measured by `rs-worst`; a test pins the node cap × steps per update that bounds it); `KH-A6` *(new, M15)* the two rows of billows spin in opposite senses and the line shows it flat: `Hot` for positive spin, `Cold` for negative, the brighter step from 0.45, the same ink across its width, nothing from 0.9 px.

### particle_accelerator  (Forces & Waves, M5, M12, M15)
- **Intent**: a collider ring where two counter-rotating bunches accelerate, meet at the detector, and burst into a shower of curling tracks.
- **Model**: ring = ellipse `R(0.8)`; bunches at angles `−π/2 + φ(t)` and `−π/2 − φ(t)` (mirror images about the vertical axis), so they meet only when `φ ≡ 0` (the detector, top) or `φ ≡ π` (a second, quieter interaction point, bottom); `dφ/dt` ramps linearly from 0.5 to 6 rad/s over 10 s; after the ramp the bunches collide at the detector each time they meet; each collision spawns 20–40 shower tracks with random momentum `p`, charge ±1, moving in a uniform magnetic field so each follows a circle of radius `r = p/B` (curling clockwise for +, counter-clockwise for −), losing momentum as `dp/dt = −0.8 p` (closed-form inward spiral, exact for any dt; non-relativistic: speed = p, radius = p/B) and fading after ≤ 2 s. Pool capped at 128. Collisions flash; the bottom interaction point only glints. Cycle 20 s; the first shower comes after the 10 s ramp.
- **Inks**: ring `Structure`, bunches `Accent` with short trails, tracks `Hot` (+) / `Cold` (−), detector `Text` label.
- **Acceptance**: `ACC-A1` the two bunches are always mirror images about the vertical axis and meet only at the two interaction points; `ACC-A2` a shower track's curvature radius equals `p/B` and its turning sign equals its charge; `ACC-A3` showers only spawn at the detector after the ramp and the pool never exceeds capacity.

### quantum_double_slit  (Forces & Waves, M13, M16)
- **Intent**: electrons from a steady source meet a wall with two slits: the wave spreads through both into a fan of bright lobes and dark nodal rays, and on the screen behind, single dots appear one at a time until the fringes emerge from the clicks. A last act aims the same beam at a thin barrier that it partly tunnels through.
- **Model**: 2-D time-dependent Schrödinger equation on a capped grid (≤ 11 000 nodes, view aspect 1–3) with ADI Crank–Nicolson (unitary), a complex tridiagonal (Thomas) solver with caller-owned scratch (CORE-R100; the line matrices of both potentials factored once per grid; M14: the sweeps solve 8 lines at a time interleaved, bit-identical to one at a time), absorbing complex potential at the edges, 2 ADI steps per update. A soft line source near the left absorber adds `S·g(y)·e^{−iφn}` after every step (`g` Gaussian, σ = 0.15 of the view height, lighting the two slits; `φ` the step's eigenphase for the carrier `k`), so a monochromatic beam drives a steady diffraction pattern; a new grid starts from the analytic incident beam and runs 2× fast for 2 s. Clicks arrive at a rate proportional to the probability current reaching the screen column (as a share of the beam's current) at positions sampled from that current (`j = 2 Im(ψ*ₓψₓ₊₁)`, clamped ≥ 0) with `ctx.rng` via `InverseCdf`; clicks accumulate (pool 4000) and a per-row click histogram glows behind them.
- **Motion**: the fan is up within ~1 s of any start or resize and stays; acts of 20 s: `interference` (full exposure, ~30 clicks/s), `one at a time` (wave held at 92 %, so the fan never reads as fading; ~10 clicks/s, the histogram glow builds the bands), `tunnelling` (the beam on a V₀ = 1.6, 2-node barrier: reflected standing wave left, weak transmitted wave right at the same exposure).
- **Resize**: grid regridded and the beam restarted from the analytic incident beam; click histogram kept in screen fractions.
- **Inks**: the wave as a soft single-hue glow, `Ramp(Aurora)` (a phosphor green): brightness `|ψ|^0.65` against the per-side peak (the source side at 55 %), a soft knee instead of a hard cut, so faint density fades smoothly to black; where the view resolves the carrier a 25 % shimmer of `Re ψ/|ψ|` rides on it, so wavefronts stream through the steady fan of lobes and dark nodal rays. Walls `Ramp(Starlight)` 111, barrier 71, screen 55; the detector strip (≈ 1/6 of the width) glows `Ramp(Starlight)` 18–170 behind the clicks by where they landed (faded in over the first 500 clicks), and the clicks are warm-white dots, 255 settling to 185. ASCII: the glow's level on the ASCII ramp, walls `|`, the strip's glow `.`/`:`.
- **M15 look** (ADR-0018; supersedes the Inks above and M14's muted `Aurora` glow): the wave is `Wave` (cyan) in three flat steps of its brightness `L = 140·vis·t·knee²·shimmer`: 90 from 30, 165 from 62, 255 from 98 (the crests), black below 30, with a 50 % carrier shimmer so the crests stream through the fan as brighter steps (as M11's `wave_interference` shows its crests). The slit wall and the barrier are drawn only at their front face, a 1-px `Structure` line (150 and 100); the screen a dashed 1-px `Dim` line (110). The detector strip is black: the fringes build from the click dots' density alone, `Star` 255 for their first 0.6 s, then 185.
- **Look** *(M16, GEN-R25; supersedes the M15 look and the Inks above)*: braille threads on black, no half-blocks. The wave is drawn as its crest lines: the wavefronts where the carrier peaks, the level `Im ψ = 0` in cells whose corners' `Re ψ` sum is positive, traced over the view's nodes by marching squares (`Dots::contour_by`, CORE-R105). Each crest piece is gated and stepped by its cell's exposed `|ψ|` (the mean of its corners, with M15's per-side peaks, tone and knee, no shimmer) in three flat `Wave` steps, 175 from 25, 230 from 45, 255 from 70 *(round 2: were 140/205/255 from 25/50/75, which left the fan faint; M15's fills were 90/165/255 from 30/62/98)*, so the lobes are bright arcs streaming out of the slits and the dark nodal rays are gaps between them; on the source side the standing wave shows as short crest bars. A view with fewer than 0.8 dots per node (crests ≈ 7 nodes apart would crowd) draws `|ψ|` iso-lines at the three steps instead. The slit wall (or barrier) is a one-dot `Structure` line at its front face (250; barrier 210), the screen a dashed one-dot `Dim` line (150) *(round 2: were 190, 140 and 110)*; clicks unchanged (`Star` 255, then 185). ASCII falls back by braille dot count.
- **Title**: `Double slit · waves of chance`; captions `interference`, `one at a time`, `tunnelling`.
- **Stylisation**: units chosen so fringes are visible; a steady monochromatic beam stands for many electrons, and single clicks are sampled from the arriving current (Born rule): the wave itself is not observable; the density is shown as a glow rather than `arg ψ` (Phase inks carry no brightness).
- **Acceptance**: `QDS-A1` the source-free ADI step preserves the norm to 1e-10 without absorbing boundaries; `QDS-A2` the fringe spacing at the screen (between the dark fringes either side of the centre) matches `λL/d` within 10 %, with the lattice's paraxial wavelength `λ = 2π/sin k`; `QDS-A3` with one slit closed the fringes vanish (visibility < 0.2 over one fringe period on the open slit's axis); `QDS-A4` the click histogram converges to the arriving current j⁺ at the screen (the sampled profile; χ² test on 4000 clicks); `QDS-A5` the share of the beam's current transmitted through the barrier falls with its width; `QDS-A6` the source settles to the analytic beam (within 10 %, steady over a carrier period), and *(changed, M16)* the fan's crests light ≥ 60 % of the cell rows next to the screen within 1.5 s of a start at 80×24, 120×40 and 200×50; `QDS-A7` every act shows the wave at ≥ 90 % of full exposure; `QDS-A8` *(changed, M16)* at 120×40 after 6 s every wave cell is `Wave` braille at one of its three steps (the upper two both showing), every other lit cell a wall, barrier, screen or click ink, and no cell is a half-block or has a background; `QDS-A9` *(new, M16)* the crest lines lie on the carrier's crests: for a plane wave `e^{ikx}` at full exposure ≥ 97 % of the wave dots away from the wall lie within 0.6 node of `kx ≡ 0 (mod 2π)`; `QDS-A10` *(new, M16 round 2)* the fan reads bright: at 120×40 after 6 s at least half of the wave's dots are in cells of level ≥ 230.

### radioactive_decay  (Forces & Waves, M6, M12, M15)
- **Intent**: a lattice of unstable nuclei flickering through a decay chain while live population curves trace the exponential law.
- **Model**: chain `A → B → C` (C stable) with half-lives `T_A = 8 s`, `T_B = 3 s`; every nucleus decays within a step with probability `1 − 2^{−dt/T}` (exact for any dt); each decay emits a short-lived α/β track particle (`ParticlePool`) and a flash. Lattice on the left (≈ 62 % width, one nucleus per cell or per 2 cells for readability), plot panel on the right (only if ≥ 24×10 cells) with measured `N_A, N_B, N_C` and the analytic Bateman curves `N_A = N₀ 2^{−t/T_A}`, `N_B = N₀ λ_A/(λ_B − λ_A)(e^{−λ_A t} − e^{−λ_B t})`. Reset when `N_A + N_B < 2 %` of N₀ (≈ 50.6 s). A→B→C within one step is sampled exactly from the 3×3 transition matrix. The panel itself must be ≥ 24×10 (≈ terminals ≥ 63 columns); a resize starts a fresh run.
- **Inks**: A `Hot`, B `Accent`, C `Dim`, tracks `Particle`, curves: measured points in their species ink (A `Hot`, B `Accent`, C `Dim`, legend likewise), analytic Bateman curves `Structure`, axes/labels `Text`.
- **Acceptance**: `RAD-A1` per-step decay probability is `1 − 2^{−dt/T}` and composes exactly (`p(dt₁) ∘ p(dt₂) = p(dt₁ + dt₂)`); `RAD-A2` a seeded 20 000-nucleus run keeps `N_A(t)` within 4σ of `N₀ 2^{−t/T_A}` and `N_B(t)` within 4σ of Bateman; `RAD-A3` `N_A + N_B + N_C = N₀` at all times.

### rayleigh_benard  (Forces & Waves, M21)
- **Intent**: a fluid layer heated from below and cooled from above turns over in convection rolls: hot plumes rise, cold sheets sink, and the rolls slowly shift.
- **Model**: 2-D Boussinesq convection with free-slip walls as a truncated Galerkin (Saltzman/Lorenz-type) Fourier model: streamfunction `ψ = Σ aₖ(t) sin(kπx/L) sin(πz)` and temperature anomaly `θ = Σ bₖ(t) cos(kπx/L) sin(πz) + Σ cⱼ(t) sin(2jπz)` over `m ≤ 10` vertical and `k ≤ round(8L)` horizontal modes, integrated with RK4 (sub-stepped); Rayleigh number above critical (`Ra_c = 27π⁴/4`) so rolls form and, with several modes, wander. Velocity `(u, w) = (−∂ψ/∂z, ∂ψ/∂x)` is analytic, so tracers advect exactly through it. Amplitudes start small from `ctx.rng` so the rolls visibly form.
- **Motion**: the rolls grow from rest in a few seconds, then the plumes sway; tracers loop the rolls.
- **Resize**: the box spans the screen with its aspect from the size; per-column/row sin/cos tables rebuilt (cheap per-cell evaluation); tracers kept in box units.
- **Look** *(GEN-R26)*: the horizontal temperature anomaly `θ′ = θ − ⟨θ⟩ₓ` (the `k ≥ 1` modes, so plumes show, not the mean profile) as fixed-threshold stipple, density `0.30 · clamp((|θ′| − θ₀)/(θ_ref − θ₀), 0, 1)` with floor `θ₀ = 0.18` and `θ_ref = 0.35` (only plume cores; the mixed interior stays black), `Hot` warm, `Cold` cool; 6–40 tracer threads (scaled with area) with a short afterglow, coloured by speed (`Wave` slow → `Particle` fast, two or three flat steps); the hot floor and cold lid as 1-px `Hot` and `Cold` threads.
- **Title**: `Convection · heated from below`; no captions.
- **Stylisation**: a low-order modal model of convection, not a resolved simulation; free-slip walls; time and Rayleigh number in model units.
- **Acceptance**: `RB-A1` below `Ra_c` a small perturbation decays and above it grows (linear stability of the model); `RB-A2` the velocity field is divergence-free (numerically, ≤ 1e-9); `RB-A3` once rolls form, the mean `w·θ` is positive (hot fluid rises: Nusselt > 1); `RB-A4` tracers never leave the box; `RB-A5` stipple dots are `Hot` only where `θ′ > 0` and `Cold` only where `θ′ < 0`, none where `|θ′| < θ₀`, density ≤ 0.30.
- **As built**: Ra = 100 Ra_c, Pr = 10 (cool water); an exact-quadrature pseudo-spectral Galerkin projection with integrating-factor RK4; 0.006 model times per second. The flow opens on the growing (2, 1) eigenmode (a central warm plume between two counter-rotating rolls, temperature amplitude 0.12) plus 0.005 noise on the other modes, so rolls show within the first second and then wander. About 1 tracer per 330 cells; 20-step afterglow, the oldest quarter dimmer; speed tiers at 0.8 and 1.4 × the RMS speed. The box is inset 2 columns at each side and 1 row from the top and from the title row (no inset below 12 × 6 cells), its aspect from the box (clamped 0.5–4).

### strange_attractors  (Forces & Waves, M10, M12, M15)
- **Intent**: a slowly turning gallery of five classic chaotic flows: Lorenz's butterfly, Aizawa's shell, Thomas's labyrinth, Halvorsen's propeller and Rössler's band. Each is drawn by a swarm of particles leaving short glowing threads on the braille lattice, so the flow itself builds the shape instead of a plotted curve. Every 12 s the swarm flows into the next attractor, with no cut and no fade to black. Each attractor is framed to its own projected extent, so it fills about 0.88 of the height (or of the width, if that is tighter). Each is also seen from its own best elevation: Halvorsen from above its 3-fold axis, so the three blades read, with the frame following the blades so the figure stays centred as they turn. Threads are continuous at every size and the particle count grows only linearly with the lattice, so 200×50 gains resolution but not clutter. The eye goes first to the bright dense cores (the Lorenz wing eyes, the Aizawa tube), then follows the threads outward. A single corner label is the only text.
- **Model**: model time in *tu*. Gallery: Lorenz (1963) `ẋ = σ(y−x), ẏ = x(ρ−z) − y, ż = xy − βz`, σ = 10, ρ = 28, β = 8/3. Aizawa (1982, Langford-type torus flow) `ẋ = (z−b)x − dy, ẏ = dx + (z−b)y, ż = c + az − z³/3 − (x²+y²)(1+ez) + fzx³`, (a,b,c,d,e,f) = (0.95, 0.7, 0.6, 3.5, 0.25, 0.1). Thomas (1999) `ẋ = sin y − bx` (cyclic), b = 0.18 (λ₁ ≈ 0.038). The popular b = 0.19 is a limit cycle, and at the often-quoted b = 0.1998 and 0.208186 orbits stay on one of several coexisting off-centre attractors. Halvorsen (Sprott 2003) `ẋ = −ax − 4y − 4z − y²` (cyclic), a = 1.27 (λ₁ ≈ 0.82); the popular a = 1.89 is periodic (λ₁ ≈ 0). Rössler (1976) `ẋ = −y − z, ẏ = x + ay, ż = b + z(x − c)`, a = b = 0.2, c = 5.7 (λ₁ ≈ 0.071). Starts are fixed in-basin constants, never random: Lorenz (1, 1, 1), Aizawa (0.1, 0, 0), Thomas (0.1, 0, 0), Halvorsen (−1, 0, 0), Rössler (1, 1, 0). Aizawa has a coexisting non-chaotic attractor: from (0.2, 0.1, 0.5), λ₁ ≈ 0 and the z extent is only 82 % of the chaotic one. Each particle is advanced in f64 with `ode::integrate` (RK4) at `h_max` = 0.01 / 0.02 / 0.1 / 0.005 / 0.02 tu, at a model rate `r_k` = 0.33 / 0.69 / 6.5 / 0.31 / 1.6 tu per simulated second. These rates are measured so the mean normalised speed is 1.2 box half-widths/s for every attractor (the peak is ≈ 9/s in Rössler's fold). That is ≤ 2 substeps per particle per 1/60 s. Normalisation: `x̃ = Q_k(x − c_k)/s_k`, where `c_k` is the reference box centre and `s_k` its largest half-extent: Lorenz (0, 0, 24.5)/26.0, Aizawa (0, 0, 0.74)/1.49, Thomas 0/4.2, Halvorsen −3.54·(1,1,1)/9.8, Rössler (1.17, −1.47, 11.36)/11.35. World position `p = x̃/ρ_k` with `ρ_k = max|x̃|` = 1.24 / 1.02 / 1.13 / 1.32 / 1.38, so `|p| ≤ 1` and the perspective stays bounded. `Q_k` maps native z (Lorenz, Aizawa, Rössler) or the (1,1,1) axis (Thomas, Halvorsen) onto the camera's up axis +y, so yaw spins the last two about their 3-fold axis. Ensemble: N = 1024 particles per attractor, sampled from one scout trajectory. The scout starts at the fixed start plus a seeded 10⁻³ Gaussian jitter, burns in for 40 s·r_k, and takes a sample every 0.15 s·r_k. Samples are stored at bit-reversed indices, so every power-of-two prefix is spread along the scout. Two slots hold the current and next ensemble. The next slot is filled during the first 2 s of each hold (≤ 400 scout steps per update, with any remainder finished at the morph boundary); a scout that escapes restarts from the fixed start offset by 10⁻³·i on every axis at its i-th restart, burn-in included, so no transient sample is kept; after 8 restarts it fills the rest of its slot by repeating the samples it has (the start if none), so filling always terminates (unreachable with today's in-basin starts, but `run` is called with an unbounded budget). Morph: at morph start each prefix is put in pairing order: 8 equal-count azimuth sectors about the vertical axis, each cut into 4 equal-count height bands, each band ordered by cylindrical radius (in place with `sort_unstable_by`, ties broken by index). Equal counts keep sector j of one attractor paired with sector j of the other however unevenly their mass is spread (Lorenz's wings). The next slot's prefix is then permuted onto the current slot's indices, so the points on screen are never re-indexed, and point i pairs a_i ↔ b_i. Both slots integrate during the morph, so the pairs decorrelate within about a second, and a plain lerp of two uncorrelated clouds is narrower (its spread falls to `√((1−w)² + w²)` ≈ 0.71 at w = ½): the swarm knotted to 0.65–0.83 of its height mid-morph. So the displayed point is `p_i,j = m_j + g_j·(lerp(a_i,j, b_i,j, w) − m_j)` per world axis j, with `w = smoothstep(s)`, `m_j = lerp(mean a_j, mean b_j, w)`, and `g_j` clamped to [1, √2] so that the blend's spread `(⟨|p_j − m_j|^16⟩)^{1/16}` equals the lerp of the two ensembles' spreads. The factor is measured after every morph step (O(N_eff), allocation-free), and the endpoints w = 0 and 1 are exact. A variance match (power 2) overshot: the extremes decorrelate last (pairs at the top of both stay there while the bulk mixes) and a blend has thinner tails than the shapes it averages, so Aizawa → Thomas spilled past the frame (80×24: 5.6 edge-row cells per morph frame, against 0.07 in holds, and 0.7 at power 16). One gain for all axes turns uncorrelated extremes into corners the shapes never have (4.8), and an order statistic jumped 0.3 world units in one update when a fast particle overtook. At s = 1 the next slot becomes current. Blow-up guard: a particle that is non-finite or has `|x̃|∞ > 3` is replaced by a clone of particle `(i + N_eff/2) mod N_eff` of the simulated prefix, or, if that has escaped too, of the next finite particle (the fixed start only if all have escaped, since it lies off the attractor, e.g. at Lorenz's origin saddle), plus a seeded 10⁻⁶·s_k jitter, so the clone separates by chaos instead of shadowing its source forever. Camera: `geom::Camera` with yaw `2πt/36 s`, pitch `p_k + 0.10 sin(2πt/29 s)` with `p_k` = 0.20 / 0.48 / 0.55 / 1.35 / 0.50 (Halvorsen from nearly above its 3-fold axis, so the three blades read; Aizawa, the seed-42 opening, from far enough above that its shell's loops open into rings around the tube instead of a tangle, which at 0.35 made it the least legible member at 80×24), and distance 4.5. Framing uses each attractor's 99.5th-percentile projected box over the camera loop (yaw every 5°, pitch p_k ± 0.10), perspective included, in world units with screen y down: `Frame_k = (x, top, bottom, swing)` with `|x|/ASPECT ≤ x` and `top ≤ y − swing·cos 3ψ ≤ bottom` at camera yaw ψ: Lorenz (0.90, −0.66, 0.66, 0), Aizawa (1.01, −0.82, 0.94, 0), Thomas (0.87, −1.01, 0.98, 0), Halvorsen (0.94, −0.80, 0.92, 0.12), Rössler (0.65, −0.74, 1.02, 0). The swing follows Halvorsen's blades: seen from above they turn like a triangle, and every 120° of yaw a tip points up, then down, swinging the box midpoint by ±0.17 half-heights while the centroid stays put. A fixed centre left 4 blank rows on one side and none on the other within one hold at 80×24 (9 of 49 at 200×50), and following it also narrows the frame from 1.89 to 1.72. The others swing ≤ 0.06 half-heights with no 3-fold harmonic. The stage is the rows above the label (all rows when it is hidden). The fit is `scale_k = min(0.88·rows/(bottom − top), 0.92·w/(2·ASPECT·x))` rows per world unit, and the frame's midpoint `(top + bottom)/2 + swing·cos 3ψ` sits on the stage's middle row, `rows/2`. Rare excursions beyond the 99.5 % box may be clipped at the frame edge. During a morph `p_k` and the frame midpoint are lerped by w and `scale_k` log-lerped. The horizontal centre is `w/2`, and dot coordinates are cell coordinates × (2, 4). Density: `Grid<f32>` at `Dots::dot_size()`. Each update multiplies it by `e^{−dt/τ}` (τ = 0.1 s), then deposits `w = (0.6 + 0.4·near)·(1 − 0.2·sin πw_morph)` (near = (depth + 1)/2; the second factor dims a morph lightly at its middle, where the lerped velocity already shortens and fades the blended threads) on every dot of the segment from each particle's previous projected dot to its current one (`geom::line`, start dot excluded). A segment longer than 16 dots deposits only its end dot; this happens after a morph start, a blow-up replacement, a resize or a large dt. A dot is lit when `D ≥ D_min = 0.08`, so threads are ≈ 0.25 s of flow long and unbroken at any size. Because thread length in dots grows with the lattice, the prefix grows only linearly: `N_eff = clamp(pow2_floor(6·min(h, w/2)), 64, 1024)`, which is 128 at 80×24 and 256 at 200×50 and keeps coverage near 30 % of the silhouette. Chaos reference: Lorenz λ₁ = 0.9056 (Viswanath 1998; Sprott 2003), with Σλ = −(σ + 1 + β) = −41/3.
- **Motion**: particles stream at ≈ 1.2 half-widths/s and the camera turns 10°/s, so the frame changes from the first step (GEN-R9). The Timeline is `hold` 8 s plus `morph` 4 s per attractor, in the order Lorenz → Aizawa → Thomas → Halvorsen → Rössler, a 60 s loop. The run starts at the beginning of a seeded attractor's hold, so every 15 s scene visit (the default `interval`) includes a morph. During a morph the cloud flows as one body, keeping its size: the old silhouette dissolves while the new one condenses, framing and elevation glide to the new attractor's, and the label switches at s = 0.5.
- **Resize**: no simulation state depends on the display (ADR-0012 needs no lattice here). Ensembles, timeline, time and camera phase are kept. A resize reallocates only the density grid and `Dots` (zeroed), recomputes centre, scale and `N_eff`, and forgets each particle's previous dot. It then rebuilds the threads by replaying each particle's flow backwards (the reversed field, integrated forwards, weight, stretch and camera held) over the lifetime of a unit deposit, `τ·ln(1/D_min)` ≈ 0.253 s = 16 updates of 1/60 s, depositing each replayed segment with the decay it would have had since. The first frame after a resize therefore already shows whole threads, at ≥ 0.85 of the following hold's mean lit cells (measured 0.93; seeds 0–23 ≥ 0.90). The reversed flow expands volumes, but over (16/60)·r_k tu (0.08–1.7 tu; stiff Lorenz only 0.09) it amplifies rounding by at most ≈ e^{|λ₃|·span} ≈ 10× (Rössler); a backward orbit that leaves the box stops its replay. A resize during a morph defers the `N_eff` change to the next hold, so the pairing stays intact. Zero-area sizes skip projection and plotting, and the fit is clamped to a positive minimum so the log-lerped scale never becomes NaN.
- **Inks**: threads `Role::Seq` with level `ink::seq(ln(1 + D/D_min)/ln(1 + 4/D_min))`: violet tails, orange heads, and pale-yellow dense cores where threads overlap. A cell takes its brightest dot (CORE-R41). Label `Text` 150.
- **ASCII**: `Dots` fallback by dot count (`.`, `:`, `+`, `#`). At 80×24 (Lorenz, t ≈ 1 s) the figure spans ≈ 30 columns × 18 rows: one wing face-on as nested `+`/`#` loops, the other nearly edge-on as a `#` blade, which turns into the classic two-lobed view as the camera swings. Halvorsen seen from above is a three-bladed `+#` triangle. Rössler is a `:` band with one `+` fold. The label uses its ASCII variant.
- **Label**: the attractor's name is `caption()` (GEN-R12), so the title reads `Strange attractor · Lorenz`. Only its parameter set is drawn, right-aligned on row h−1 (GEN-R13), static strings only (GEN-R11): `σ=10 ρ=28 β=8/3` (ASCII `s=10 r=28 b=8/3`), `b=0.18` (Thomas), `a=1.27` (Halvorsen), `a=.2 b=.2 c=5.7` (Rössler), and none for Aizawa: its six (41 characters) do not fit, and quoting two of six would read as its only parameters. Every set is restrained (≤ 16 characters), Unicode and ASCII variants have the same length, and a set is shown whole within `title::readout_room` or not at all. It is dropped when `w < 12` or `h < 8`; while the label can show, row h−1 takes no dots.
- **Perf**: estimated at 200×50 (N_eff = 256) for `update(1/60)` + `render`. Integration: 256 × ≤ 2 RK4 steps (×2 during a morph), ≈ 0.02 ms. Projection and segment deposits ≈ 0.02 ms. A fused decay, threshold and `Dots::set` pass over 80 k dots ≈ 0.08 ms. Scout ≤ 400 steps ≈ 0.01 ms. Blit of 10 k cells ≈ 0.03 ms. The morph stretch is three O(N_eff) passes over both slots per update. Total ≈ 0.2 ms (measured 0.24 ms per hold update and 0.27 ms per morph update at 200×50 on a loaded machine). The worst case is dt = 0.25 during a Thomas morph (17 substeps × 2 slots), ≈ 0.3 ms at N_eff 256 and ≈ 1 ms at N_eff 1024 (terminals ≥ 342×171). The first resize fills the current slot synchronously (≤ 13 k RK4 steps). Allocation-free after warm-up: particle, previous-dot, pairing and stretch buffers are sized at build, the density grid and `Dots` in resize, the Timeline in the first resize, and sorting uses `sort_unstable_by` (the stable sort allocates).
- **Acceptance**: `ATT-A1` the tangent-vector (Benettin 1980) estimate of Lorenz λ₁ (analytic Jacobian, RK4 h = 0.005, 20 tu burn-in, T = 400 tu, mean over the starts (1,1,1), (−3,2,20) and (5,−5,30)) lies in [0.86, 0.95] (reference 0.9056; measured 0.899–0.912); `ATT-A2` from its fixed start every gallery member has λ₁ > 0.015 (h = h_max,k except Lorenz 0.005; burn-in 50 tu, Rössler 100 and Thomas 200; T = 400 for Lorenz and Halvorsen, 600 for Aizawa, 1500 for Rössler and Thomas; measured 0.91 / 0.089 / 0.039 / 0.83 / 0.071), while the periodic controls Halvorsen a = 1.89 (T = 400) and Thomas b = 0.19 (T = 1500) from the same starts give |λ₁| < 0.005 (measured −0.003 to 0.002; ≈ 1 s in a debug build, the slowest test here); `ATT-A3` field fidelity: Lorenz vanishes at C± = (±√72, ±√72, 27) (1e-12); analytic Jacobians match central differences at 64 seeded points (1e-6 relative), and their traces equal the analytic divergences −41/3, 2(z−b) + a − z² − e(x²+y²) + fx³, −3b, −3a and a + x − c; Lorenz is exactly odd in (x, y), and Thomas and Halvorsen are exactly cyclic, f(Cx) = C f(x); `ATT-A4` for seeds 0–7, each 1024-point scout ensemble has max|x̃|∞ ≤ 1.05, per-axis extents ≥ 0.9× the reference box (Lorenz 0.8×: a 1024 × 0.05 tu = 51 tu scout rarely reaches the outer spiral; ### three_body  (Forces & Waves, M10, M12, M15, M17)
- **Intent**: three equal suns dancing some of the rare *periodic* solutions of the three-body problem: the figure-8, where all three chase each other along one curve; Lagrange's equilateral triangle spinning and breathing; Broucke's A1 and R4; and Šuvakov & Dmitrašinović's moth I. Each glowing head draws its own comet trail, exactly one period long, so the orbit is sketched once and then keeps refreshing. The loop is a small gallery of 250 years of celestial mechanics, and it is honest about chaos: each orbit restarts from its published initial conditions long before any instability can show.
- **Model**: planar Newtonian three-body problem, `G = m₁ = m₂ = m₃ = 1`, `r̈ᵢ = Σ_{j≠i} (rⱼ − rᵢ)/|rⱼ − rᵢ|³`, unsoftened. The symmetric (Newton 3) pair loop conserves `P` and `L` to rounding. KDK leapfrog (`ode::verlet_step`, CORE-R44) runs on a fixed per-orbit substep lattice `hᵢ = Tᵢ/(480·qᵢ)`, `qᵢ = ⌈4096·Tᵢ/480⌉` (q = 54, 48, 54, 47, 128, so `hᵢ ≤ 1/4096`), and one trail sample is pushed every `qᵢ` substeps. Each update takes `⌊τ·kᵢ/hᵢ⌋ − n_done` whole substeps, where `τ` is the time since the current `run` started, so the state is a pure function of the substep count and does not depend on the frame rate. `verlet_integrate` is not used because it chooses its own equal substeps. State is `pos, vel, acc: [f64; 6]`. The orbits (ICs COM-centred with `P = 0`) are below; `k` is model time units per simulated second and `n` is the number of periods shown:
  - **figure-8** (Moore 1993; Chenciner & Montgomery 2000; ICs by Simó): `r₁ = −r₂ = (0.97000436, −0.24308753)`, `r₃ = 0`, `v₃ = (−0.93240737, −0.86473146)`, `v₁ = v₂ = −v₃/2`, `T = 6.32591398`, `E = −1.287142`, `L = 0`; KAM-stable (Simó 2002); `k = 1`, `n = 3`.
  - **Lagrange** (1772), homographic elliptic variant with `e = 0.3`: the bodies start at apocentre at `90° + 120°·i` on the unit circle, with perpendicular velocity `v_a = √(μ(1−e))` and `μ = Gm/√3` (the net pull on each body is `μ/r²` toward the COM). Each body therefore follows a Kepler ellipse about the COM with `a = 1/1.3`, `r_p = 0.538462`, `T = 2π√(a³√3) = 5.578856`, `E = −1.125833`, `L = 1.907172`. Routh's stability condition `27(m₁m₂ + m₂m₃ + m₃m₁) < (Σm)²` fails for equal masses (81 > 9), so the configuration is linearly unstable (Gascheau 1843, Routh 1875); in the circular case the growth rate is `ω/√2`, ×85 per period. `k = 1`, `n = 2`.
  - **Broucke A1** (Broucke 1975; Hénon 1976; ICs from the Šuvakov–Dmitrašinović gallery): `x = (−0.9892620043, 2.2096177241, −1.2203557197)`, `y = 0`, `v_x = 0`, `v_y = (1.9169244185, 0.1910268738, −2.1079512924)`, `T = 6.283247` (refined from the published 6.283213, see below), `E = −0.854131`, `L = 1.098206`; `k = 1`, `n = 2`.
  - **Broucke R4**: `x = (0.8733047091, −0.6254030288, −0.2479016803)`, `v_y = (1.0107764436, −1.6833533458, 0.6725769022)`, `T = 5.396075` (refined from the published 5.396), `E = −2.054282`, `L = 1.768757`; `k = 1`, `n = 2`.
  - **Refined periods**: for A1, R4 and moth I, `T` is the period that minimises the return error of the quoted ICs on the orbit's own leapfrog lattice (TB-A3), so each trail closes on itself. The tabulated periods are rounded, and they do not match the truncated ICs: at the published values the lattice return error is 1.1e-4 (A1), 2.5e-4 (R4) and 3.6e-4 (moth). For A1 and R4 that error is a phase lag. ### vortex_street  (Forces & Waves, M10, M12, M15, M16)
- **Intent**: flow past a cylinder at Re ≈ 90–150 (≈ 125–140 on the reference lattices) sheds a von Kármán vortex street. That is the regime of real 2-D laminar shedding: onset is at Re ≈ 47, and 3-D mode A appears above Re ≈ 190. A calm inflow comes in from the left and meets a solid round cylinder 15 % of the way across (never closer than 1.65 D to the inlet). Two thin shear layers peel off its top and bottom, flap, and roll up into alternating eddies, clockwise (cyan) in the upper row and counter-clockwise (orange) in the lower row, drifting right at ≈ 0.85 U. The eye lands on the cylinder and then follows the wake. Only the vorticity field, the cylinder and one corner label are drawn; the irrotational flow is the terminal's own background. At 80×24 the cylinder is ≈ 7 columns × 3½ rows centred near column 11.3 and row 11.5, with 3–4 separate eddies in view in a band across the middle rows. At 200×50 the framing is the same, and the eddies have soft graded cores and the shear layers read as fine filaments.
- **Model**: D2Q9 lattice Boltzmann (Qian, d'Humières & Lallemand 1992) in lattice units (Δx = Δt = 1). Streaming is `f_i(x + c_i, t + 1) = f_i*(x, t)`, with equilibrium `f_i^eq = w_i ρ (1 + 3 c_i·u + 4.5 (c_i·u)² − 1.5 u²)`, weights `w = 4/9, 1/9 (×4), 1/36 (×4)` and `c_s² = 1/3`. **Collision**: two-relaxation-time (TRT; Ginzburg, Verhaeghe & d'Humières 2008), `f_i* = f_i − ω⁺(f_i⁺ − f_i^{eq+}) − ω⁻(f_i⁻ − f_i^{eq−})`, where `f_i^± = (f_i ± f_ī)/2` pairs opposite directions, `ω⁺ = 1/τ`, `ν = (τ − ½)/3`, and `ω⁻ = 1/τ⁻` with magic parameter `Λ = (τ − ½)(τ⁻ − ½) = 1/4`. BGK (Bhatnagar, Gross & Krook 1954) is the special case `τ⁻ = τ`, i.e. TRT with `Λ = (τ − ½)² ≈ 9·10⁻⁴` at τ = 0.53; the stable velocity range collapses as Λ → 0 and BGK blows up in the under-resolved shear layers, whereas Λ = ¼ is Ginzburg's stability-optimal choice (Λ = 3/16 would instead place the bounce-back wall exactly halfway for Poiseuille flow). TRT has the same ν(τ) and a similar cost. The kernel is a fused pull-scheme stream+collide in f32 over SoA planes. Populations are stored as `f_i − w_i` (Lehmann et al. 2022) so the O(Ma²) deviations keep their precision, and ρ, uₓ, u_y are written out as a by-product. **Lattice**: the lattice does not depend on terminal size. The display's square-pixel aspect `A = w/(2h)` is clamped to [1, 4] and widened by the hidden sponge (`A/0.9`). The lattice is the capped shape of exactly that snapped aspect, `grid::lattice_for_aspect(1.12^k, MAX_NODES = 10 240)` (CORE-R69), whatever the display's size, so every terminal in one aspect bucket runs identical physics: 150×68 at 200×50 and 134×76 at 80×24. The view shows lattice columns 1 … W_vis − 1 with `W_vis = W − ⌈0.1 W⌉`, which hides the forced inlet column and the sponge; it is sampled into the `Pixels` by a `grid::SampleMap`. For display aspects in [1, 4] its aspect is within 7 % of the display's, so the cylinder stays round; outside that range the view is stretched. **Geometry**: `D = clamp(round(min(0.14 H, W_vis/9)), 6, 16)` (blockage ≤ 0.14; the W_vis/9 bound only binds on square displays), centre `(x_c, y_c) = (max(round(0.15 W_vis), round(1.65 D)), (H − 1)/2)`, staircase mask. The `1.65 D` floor keeps a calm run-up of ≥ 1.15 D between the inlet and the cylinder's front on every display: at aspect 1 (80×40, 100×50, a vertical split of 200×50; lattice 107×95) 15 % alone gave D = 13 and x_c = 14, the front only ≈ 4 nodes past the inlet sponge, and the inflow filled with a persistent haze; the floor gives D = 11, x_c = 18, as at 80×24. 80×24 and 200×50 are unchanged. `(H − 1)/2` is the domain's exact middle, so the mask and the drawn disc are symmetric about it (the probe row is `round(y_c)`). y is periodic, so the domain is a column of cylinders H ≈ 7 D apart; the visible street is ≈ 12 D long, room for 3–4 shed eddies after the formation region (with D = 0.19 H the view held ≈ 8.6 D × 5.3 D, one street wavelength, and the eddies stretched into full-height banners). The inlet (column 0) is set every step to `f^eq(ρ_B, u_pot)` of the undisturbed potential flow past the cylinder and its images at y ± H (the inlet is only ≈ 2 D upstream, so a uniform inlet would shear the deflected stream and shed a vorticity sheet into the calm inflow), with its Bernoulli density `ρ_B = 1 + 1.5 (U² − |u_pot|²)`, the same as the start state, so the inlet forces no pressure mismatch (with ρ = 1 it was ≈ 0.004 too low on the centre line, where the flow 1.65 D upstream has slowed to ≈ 0.91 U; harmless, but inconsistent). Columns 1–3 get a ramped (≤ 0.25) relaxation toward each node's own equilibrium, which raises the viscosity there without imposing a velocity and damps the τ = 0.53 even-mode checkerboard the forced inlet column would otherwise feed. The outlet (column W − 1) copies column W − 2. Over the hidden last ⌈0.1 W⌉ columns a sponge (Israeli & Orszag 1981) adds relaxation toward the uniform stream `f^eq(1, (U(t), 0))`, with strength ramping quadratically from 0 to 0.25. The cylinder wall uses halfway bounce-back (Ladd 1994). **Parameters**: `τ = 0.53` (ν = 0.01) and `U₀ = 0.125` on every lattice, so `Re = U₀ D/ν = 12.5 D`. The node cap keeps D ≤ 12 (D ≥ 13 would need H ≥ 90 and W ≥ 125, over 10 240 nodes), so Re ≤ 150. The inflow Mach number is `U₀√3 = 0.217` (0.234 at the breath's peak), but the shoulders reach |u| ≈ 1.7 U (local Ma ≈ 0.4) and ρ swings by ≈ ±6 % (see Watchdog): the flow is weakly compressible, and its shedding frequency is validated against the literature by VOR-A5. The reference lattices give D = 10, Re = 125 (200×50) and D = 11, Re = 137.5 (80×24 and the square 107×95 lattice); narrow displays reach Re = 150 (D ≥ 12), and the widest bucket (D = 7) gives 87.5. **Breathing inlet**: the inlet speed is `U(t) = U₀·breath(t)`, `breath(t) = 1 + 0.08 sin(2π t/47 s)`, in the scene's own simulated time (lattice steps / 270); the inlet profile and the outlet sponge's target follow it, set once per `update` (quasi-static: < 0.1 % U per frame). Shedding frequency and eddy spacing drift visibly over a long visit, Re stays within [0.92, 1.08]·Re₀ ⊂ (47, 190), and the peak Ma is 0.234. **Start**: the flow starts from an analytic street, so a street exists at t = 0. The background is potential flow past the cylinder, `u = U(1 − R²(x² − y²)/r⁴, −2R² x y/r⁴)` with R = D/2, which is rational and irrotational and avoids an impulsive jolt at the wall. On top of it sit two rows of Kaufmann–Scully vortices, `v_θ = Γ r/(2π(r² + r_c²))` (rational, so no libm), with `Γ = −1.8 U D` in the upper row (clockwise) and `+1.8 U D` in the lower row, and `r_c = 0.35 D`. The streamwise spacing is `a = 4.3 D` (≈ 0.85 U/f at St ≈ 0.2) and the row gap is `b = 0.281 a` (von Kármán's stable ratio). Each vortex is summed with its periodic images at y ± H. The rows run from `x_c + 1.5 D` + a seeded offset in `[0, a/2)` to the outlet, with a seeded leading row (together every phase in `[0, a)`, always with an eddy in the near wake). A zero-net-circulation start-up pair (Kelvin) is added: a bound circulation 1.5 U D on the cylinder and the opposite eddy at (0.9 D, ±0.3 D) on the leading row's side, so the wake sheds at full amplitude within ≈ 4 s instead of growing its asymmetry for ≈ 10 s. Both are windowed toward the periodic seam, where the truncated image sums would otherwise shed vorticity sheets, and toward the inlet, which imposes the undisturbed flow; a window `w` multiplying an irrotational field creates vorticity `w′(x)·v` wherever it varies, so each has its own. The street's smoothstep eases in from the inlet to `x_c + D/2`. The pair's is 1 from `x_c − D` on and eases in from the inlet ahead of it, where the pair's field is small: under the street's window, steepest just in front of the cylinder where the bound vortex is strong, it left a vorticity sheet that took ≈ 120 steps to wash through. Initially ρ follows Bernoulli, `ρ = 1 + 1.5 (U² − |u|²)` (δp = ½(U² − |u|²), δρ = δp/c_s²), which is exact in the irrotational flow, plus each Scully core's cyclostrophic excess `δp_k = −Γ_k² r_c²/(8π²(r_k² + r_c²)²)` (street vortices with their images and the start-up eddy, times the square of their window; zero for the point bound vortex), because Bernoulli does not hold across the rotational cores: it gives a core pressure that climbs back to p∞ at the centre, where the cyclostrophic `−Γ²/(8π²(r² + r_c²))` is lowest (≈ 0.016 in ρ at D = 10, ≈ 70 % of 1.5 U²). So the start carries the pressure its velocity field implies instead of launching an acoustic pulse; u = 0 and ρ = 1 in the solid, and `f = f^eq(ρ, u)`. The first `resize` then pre-rolls 160 lattice steps (≈ x_c/U, one flush of the run-up; ≈ 1.6 M lattice updates, a one-off 12–20 ms on a release scene switch), so the first frame is past the slip sheet the potential flow leaves on the no-slip staircase, the start-up pressure pulse and the inlet's adjustment (with 40 steps the cylinder wore a speckled halo and a thin vorticity sheet crossed the calm inflow for ≈ 0.3 s). **Watchdog**: once per simulated second, any non-finite value or ρ outside [0.7, 1.3] re-initialises the flow from the analytic street. It never fires in normal operation (VOR-A5, VOR-A6, VOR-A7: 45 s at five displays and at the D = 12, Re₀ = 150 lattice; 150 s runs at every extreme lattice gave ρ ∈ [0.941, 1.037] and |u|max ≤ 1.70 U).
- **Motion**: `STEPS_PER_S = 270` lattice steps per simulated second: 4.5 per 60 Hz frame through a fractional accumulator, and at most 68 per `update` at dt = 0.25. The shedding period `P ≈ D/(St U)` is ≈ 400–450 steps at the reference lattices (St ≈ 0.20–0.21 on the free stream), or ≈ 1.5–1.7 s. Eddies advect ≈ 25–29 lattice nodes/s (≈ 0.85 U), which is ≈ 40 columns/s at 200×50 and ≈ 18 columns/s at 80×24, so motion is obvious within 1 s. Without forcing the flow would be an exact limit cycle (frames 25 s apart nearly identical); the 47 s breathing inlet makes the shedding period and eddy spacing drift by ±8 %, so a long visit evolves. It loops forever without a Timeline, and each seed opens on a different street phase.
- **Resize**: `lattice_size(display)` decides everything. If the lattice is unchanged (same aspect bucket), only the view mapping and `Pixels` are rebuilt and the populations stay bit-identical. On a bucket change, ρ, uₓ and u_y are resampled onto the new lattice with `grid::resample_bilinear` (ADR-0012); every lattice runs at the same U₀, so the moments need no rescaling. Solid nodes of the new cylinder get `u = 0`, `FlowParams` are recomputed, `f = f^eq(ρ, u)` is rebuilt and the probe history is cleared. Non-equilibrium populations are never resampled. The first resize initialises the flow from the analytic street and pre-rolls it. Every buffer (lattice planes, vorticity grid, `SampleMap`, `Pixels`) is sized here. The view is refreshed at the end of both `resize` and `update`, so `render` only blits and writes the label.
- **Inks**: vorticity `ω = ∂ₓu_y − ∂_y uₓ` (y up on screen) is taken by central differences on the visible lattice and normalised as `x = ω D/(2.5 U)`. It is soft-clipped with a rational tanh-like squash, `s = clamp(x(27 + x²)/(27 + 9x²), −1, 1)` (`ink::soft_clip`), which is odd, monotone and exactly 1 at |x| = 3, so no transcendental function runs per pixel. The vorticity grid is resampled bilinearly to pixel size, and each pixel gets `ink::div(s)` (`Role::Div`): CW (upper row, s < 0) is cyan and CCW (lower row) is orange. Pixels with |s| < 0.06 stay `Ink::BLANK`. The Scully core peak `ω_max = Γ/(π r_c²) ≈ 4.7 U/D` maps to |s| ≈ 0.975, so eddy cores are graded while the thin shear layers saturate. **Hysteresis** (as in Canny's 1986 edge detector): a 4-connected group of drawn `Div` pixels stays only if one of its pixels reaches |s| ≥ 0.12, the ASCII blank band, so a faint structure shows in both glyph modes or in neither. The faint fringes of eddies and shear layers hang on to their cores, while isolated threshold-level specks with nothing behind them are blanked: start-up transients (a detached 4-pixel strip below the cylinder at 80×24, t = 0.2 s; a dotted diagonal trailing the upper shear layer at 200×50, t = 1 s) and staircase noise, ≈ 1–2 groups per frame at any time, ≈ 0.2 % of the drawn pixels. Dropping every faint pixel not next to a strong one would instead strip ≈ 11 % of the drawn pixels at 200×50, the soft edges of the eddies. It is a flood fill from the seeds over the pixel buffer, with scratch buffers sized in `resize`. The fluid is drawn first, the hysteresis runs on it, and the cylinder goes on top; pixels centred inside the rim are not fluid, so the wall's own shear never seeds a group. Cylinder pixels are those whose centre, mapped into lattice coordinates, lies within D/2 of (x_c, y_c), so the outline is round rather than the staircase. The rim is anti-aliased by coverage `c = clamp(½ − d, 0, 1)`, with `d` the signed distance of the pixel centre outside the rim in pixel widths along the radius: `Role::Structure` level 200 where c = 1 and `120 + 80c` otherwise, i.e. 160–199 for the rim pixels whose centre is inside, and 120–159 for pixels partly covered from outside, which show the wall only where the fluid is undrawn, so they never hide the boundary layer (at 80×24 a hard 7×6-pixel disc read as a rounded square). On the upstream half (dx < 0), weak vorticity (|s| < 0.2) is not drawn within 1 node of the rim, where the staircase's corners stick out of the round mask, nor, when its sign is opposite to the boundary layer's (CW above, CCW below), within 0.6 D of the rim, where the steps' odd-even noise is washed along the wall: at Re ≲ 190 the only vorticity there is the attached boundary layer, far stronger, and opposite-signed secondary vorticity only forms behind separation. These removed single-pixel brown and navy specks at the cylinder's upstream shoulders. The label is `Role::Text` level 150.
- **ASCII**: the `Pixels::blit` closure decides per cell. A cell whose two halves are both cylinder, `Structure` at level ≥ 160 (centre inside the rim), gives `@`; so does a cell with one such half whose column centre lies within 0.35 D of x_c (the disc's top and bottom caps, columns computed in `resize`). Elsewhere a single cylinder half, and anywhere an anti-aliased outside-edge half (< 160), falls through to the other half's `Div` value, so the shoulders show the boundary layer instead of a one-pixel sliver. At 80×24 the disc is then four rows of 5, 7, 7 and 5 `@`, round at 2:1 cells; with both halves required it was two rows of 7–8, a 2:1 bar in square units inside the wake. (At 40×12 the disc is only ≈ 3.7 × 1.7 cells and stays a 2×2 block.) `s` is the mean of the `Div` halves: |s| < 0.12 is left blank, |s| < 0.25 gives `.`, s < 0 gives `x` (|s| < 0.6) or `X`, and s > 0 gives `o` or `O`, each with the averaged `Div` ink (the Unicode threshold stays 0.06; the wider blank band keeps decaying far-wake eddies from reading as clouds of dots). ### wave_interference  (Forces & Waves, M4, M12, M15, M18)
- **Intent**: two coherent point sources whose ripples add up into bright and dark bands.
- **Model**: sources at `(0.25 w, h/2 ± s/2)`, `s = 0.35 h` rows; wavelength `λ = max(3, h/6)` rows; `ω = 3` rad/s; amplitude `a = sin(k d₁ − ωt) + sin(k d₂ − ωt)`, glyph from `(a + 2)/4`.
- **Inks**: `Wave`, level ∝ `(a + 2)/4`; sources `Accent`; title `Text` (only if `h > 8`).
- **Acceptance**: `WAVE-A1` on the perpendicular bisector the amplitude is `2 sin(kd − ωt)`; `WAVE-A2` where the path difference is `λ/2` the amplitude is ≈ 0 at all times.
- **Look** *(M18, GEN-R26, orbital lobes + nodal threads; supersedes the Inks above)*: a black ground. The instantaneous field is drawn as a hydrogen-style probability cloud: braille dot `(x, y)` is lit when `stipple_keep(salt, x, y, p)` (CORE-R107) with `p = P_MAX·(a/2)²`, `a` the amplitude at the dot centre, `Hot` where `a > 0` and `Cold` where `a < 0` (the two signs of an orbital lobe), level 230 where `|a| ≥ 1.4` and 170 below, so each crest and trough is a lobe that glides outward along the bright bands without shimmer. The static nodal lines, the hyperbolas `|d₁ − d₂| = (n + ½)λ` where the waves cancel at all times, are faint dotted `Dim` 70 threads (one dot about every third dot along each line), built once per resize. Each source is a small `Sun` atom (`Dots::stipple_puff`, CORE-R108, σ 1.6 dots, peak 0.95, level 255); field dots within 3σ + 1.5 dots of a source are left dark, so the atom reads as a nucleus and is the brightest thing on screen. Nothing else: no frame, no bands of glyphs.
- **Model (M18)**: `a = 2A(s̄)·sin(k s̄ − ωt)·cos(kΔ/2)`, `s̄ = (d₁ + d₂)/2`, `Δ = d₁ − d₂`, with the common cylindrical spreading `A(s̄) = min(1, √(r₀/s̄))`, `r₀ = 1.5λ` (2-D, energy ∝ 1/r; the far-field form, common to both waves, so the nodal lines `|Δ| = (n + ½)λ` stay exact). `ω = 3` rad/s, `P_MAX = 0.75`. The stipple density is `p = F·P_MAX·(a/2)²` with a display vignette on density only, `F = 1 − smoothstep(0.55, 1, ρ)`, `ρ` the elliptical radius about the sources' midpoint (1 at the left, top and bottom frame edges and at 0.6 of the frame width right of the midpoint), so the outer third of the frame is nearly black and the rays fan out to the right; the nodal threads fade through the same `F` with fixed per-dot thresholds. Per dot, `resize` stores the phase `k s̄` in 1/256 turns as a `u8` (a negative envelope folded in as half a turn), `A·|cos(kΔ/2)|` as a `u8`, and the threshold `√(u/(F·P_MAX))/(A·|cos|)` as a `u8`; phase and envelope are stored for half the rows (the field is mirror-symmetric about the line between the sources): 2 bytes per dot, and a frame is a table lookup and a compare per dot, no trigonometry. Dots with `|cos(kΔ/2)| < 0.35` are never lit (WAVE-A4). Stylisation: the spreading uses the far-field common amplitude, exact on the bisector.
- **Acceptance** *(M18)*: `WAVE-A1` *(changed, M18)* on the perpendicular bisector the amplitude is `2A(d)·sin(kd − ωt)`; `WAVE-A3` (measured where `F = 1`, outside the sources' dark zones) the lit-dot share in a small window rises with `a²` (a window where `|a| ≈ 2` has at least 3× the lit share of one where `|a| ≈ 0.7`) and every lit field dot is `Hot` where `a > 0` and `Cold` where `a < 0`; `WAVE-A4` no field dot is lit within 0.05 λ of a nodal hyperbola, and the nodal threads lie on `|d₁ − d₂| = (n + ½)λ` within one dot; `WAVE-A5` each source is a `Sun` atom centred on its source position (centroid within 1 dot); `WAVE-A6` frames are braille-only (no half-block cells, no `o`/`0` glyphs in Unicode mode).
